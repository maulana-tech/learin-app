# ECON 110 Policy Essay: Should the City Tax Sugary Drinks?

**Due: Monday 7 December 2026, 12:00 (noon)** · Individual · 1,200 words (±10%) · 25% of the course grade

## The question
The city council proposes a tax of 0.30 per litre on sugary drinks to reduce consumption and raise revenue
for school sports. Using the concept of **price elasticity of demand**, advise the council.

## Your essay must
1. Define price elasticity of demand and explain how it is calculated.
2. Argue whether demand for sugary drinks is likely to be elastic or inelastic, using at least two
   determinants of elasticity (for example, availability of substitutes, share of income, time horizon).
3. Explain what your answer means for the two goals: will the tax cut consumption a lot, raise a lot of
   revenue, or both? Explain why the two goals can pull against each other.
4. Recommend one design change to the tax and justify it.

## Rules
- Every factual claim must cite the course reading or another source in the reference list.
- No more than two figures. Label the axes.
