# ECON 110 Principles of Microeconomics: Syllabus

Northbridge University · Fall 2026 · 3 credits
Lecturer: Dr. S. Lindqvist · Lectures Tue/Fri 08:30–10:00, Room C3.05

## Course description
How households and firms make choices: supply and demand, elasticity, consumer and producer surplus,
market structures, and the effects of taxes and price controls.

## Learning outcomes
1. Calculate the price elasticity of demand and classify it as elastic, inelastic or unit elastic.
2. Predict how a price change affects total revenue, given the elasticity.
3. Explain the determinants of elasticity with real examples.
4. Evaluate a policy (a tax or a subsidy) using elasticity.
