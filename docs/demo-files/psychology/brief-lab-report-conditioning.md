# PSY 100 Lab Report: Classical Conditioning in Everyday Life

**Due: Thursday 3 December 2026, 23:59** · Individual · 1,000–1,500 words, APA style · 25% of the course grade

## The task
Pick one everyday behaviour that you believe was learned through classical conditioning (for example,
feeling hungry when you hear a food-delivery notification). Analyse it as a conditioning experiment.

## Sections
1. **Introduction:** what classical conditioning is, with Pavlov's original procedure as the example.
2. **Analysis:** name the unconditioned stimulus, unconditioned response, conditioned stimulus and
   conditioned response in your behaviour.
3. **Prediction:** describe how you would extinguish the response, and whether spontaneous recovery
   could happen afterwards.
4. **Discussion:** one limitation of explaining your behaviour by classical conditioning alone.
5. **References:** APA 7th edition.

## Rules
Every claim about conditioning must be supported by the course reading or a cited source.
