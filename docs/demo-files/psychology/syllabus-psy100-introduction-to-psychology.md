# PSY 100 Introduction to Psychology: Syllabus

Northbridge University · Fall 2026 · 3 credits
Lecturer: Dr. H. Tanaka · Lectures Mon/Thu 15:00–16:30, Room D1.10

## Course description
A survey of how psychologists study behaviour and mind: research methods, the brain, sensation and
perception, learning, memory, development and social behaviour.

## Learning outcomes
1. Describe the procedure of classical conditioning and name its parts (US, UR, CS, CR).
2. Explain acquisition, extinction and spontaneous recovery.
3. Apply learning principles to an everyday behaviour.
4. Write a short lab report in APA style.
